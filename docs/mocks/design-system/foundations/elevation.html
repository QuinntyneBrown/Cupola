Elevation & shape

Attached layers elevate by getting lighter and gaining a hairline. Shadows exist only under detached layers — things that float over the workspace and will leave: menus, dialogs, tooltips, drag ghosts. Shape stays tight and instrument-like.

Shape#

TokenRadiusApplies to
--cp-radius-s3pxButtons, inputs, menu items, tree rows, tab bodies, kbd
--cp-radius-m6pxCards, panes, menus, banners, popups
--cp-radius-l10pxDialogs and large overlays only
--cp-radius-pill999pxChips, count badges, switch track, status dots

Nothing else is rounded. Plot canvases, gauges, imagery, and layout frames keep 3px or square corners — data surfaces should read as instruments, not bubbles.

Focus & selection treatment#

One focus ring everywhere: 2px solid --cp-color-focus-ring with 2px offset (inset −2px inside clipped containers). Selection is a fill (primary-dim), never a ring, so keyboard focus and selection remain distinguishable when they coincide (OMCT-C16-L2-03.01).

Angular mapping#

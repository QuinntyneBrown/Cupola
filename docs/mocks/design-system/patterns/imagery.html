Imagery

The imagery view renders image telemetry as a focused frame with a history strip (OMCT-C11-L2-01.01). Every overlay — timestamp, freshness, controls, compass — floats on the frame in low-alpha chips so the picture stays the subject. Timestamps and headings are always mono.

Thumbnail strip#

Every frame in the current bounds gets a thumbnail with its mono timestamp (OMCT-C11-L2-01.03). While tracking latest, the newest thumbnail is auto-focused as frames arrive; selecting an older one switches to historical mode. ← / → step the focused frame through the strip (OMCT-C11-L2-01.04); End re-joins the live edge.

Zoom, pan & the viewable-area indicator#

Wheel or the strip buttons zoom about the cursor; past 1× the image pans by drag (OMCT-C11-L2-02.01). A corner overview shows the full frame with the visible region outlined (OMCT-C11-L2-02.02) — it appears only while zoomed and vanishes at 1×.

ZOOM 4.0× · drag to pan
Zoomed 4×: the bottom-right overview outlines the visible region in primary. The crosshair button resets zoom and pan; the indicator is hidden at zoom = 1.

Image annotations#

Annotations draw as tagged rectangles on the frame (OMCT-C11-L2-03.02). Default outlines use --cp-chart-3 at 1.5px with the tag chip; the selected annotation switches to primary with resize handles. Dragging on empty image marquee-selects — or creates — a new annotation (OMCT-C11-L2-03.03).

Related telemetry readout#

Alongside the focused frame, linked parameters display their value at or before the frame's timestamp (OMCT-C11-L2-03.01) — never a later sample, so image and numbers describe the same instant.

Time-strip imagery track#

Inside a time strip, an imagery row positions thumbnails along the shared time axis (OMCT-C11-L2-01.05); selecting one focuses it in the paired imagery view.

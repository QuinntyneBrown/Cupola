Panes & panels

Every region of the shell is a pane: a condensed-caps header over a body, separated from its neighbors by draggable splitters. The inspector stacks expansion panels inside one pane; folders render their children as tiles or rows; and when a pane has nothing to show, it says so with a proper empty state instead of a blank rectangle.

Pane anatomy#

.cp-pane — surface-1 body under a 32px surface-2 head. The head holds a 14px icon, a condensed-caps label (12/16, +0.06em), a spacer, then icon buttons; last is the collapse control. Body padding is 12px (--cp-space-4); when the body is a tree, table, or canvas that manages its own gutters, the body goes flush (padding 0).

The inspector as panes#

The inspector is one pane holding a glyph tab strip and a stack of expansion panels. Tabs appear only when they apply to the current selection and keep a fixed priority order — Properties first, view-specific tabs next, Annotations last. Within a tab, sections are .cp-expand panels the operator opens and closes independently.

Splitters & collapse#

A 5px invisible hit area between panes; it tints primary on hover and while dragging. Double-click resets the split. Collapsing a pane leaves a 28px rail with its label rotated and a restore chevron — the pane is parked, not gone.
